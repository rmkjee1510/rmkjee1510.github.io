Research Overview

My work sits at the intersection of medical image computing, inverse problems, and computational pathology. I am interested in reconstruction and diagnosis pipelines that respect the physics of the acquisition device and the realities of clinical data — limited annotations, patient-level heterogeneity, and the need for calibrated, trustworthy predictions.

Primary Themes

Physics-informed reconstruction · Multimodal fusion · Weak supervision · Calibrated risk modelling

Modalities

CT / modulo sinograms · Whole-slide cytology · RGB & hyperspectral imaging · Clinical biomarkers

Thesis & Selected Projects

  • M.Tech Thesis — IIT Kharagpur Jan 2025 — Apr 2026
    Physics-Aware Learning for Modulo Sinogram Unwrapping & Reconstruction
    • Engineered a physics-informed machine learning framework to unwrap modulo sinograms, integrating CT physics and forward-projection models directly into the learning pipeline.
    • Formulated and solved the associated inverse problem by embedding physical imaging constraints into deep architectures, systematically reducing high-density artifacts and noise.
    • Improved the robustness and spatial resolution of CT reconstructions relative to traditional analytical methods.
  • Oral Cytology & OPMD Analysis — MIT Lab, SMST Jan 2025 — Jul 2025
    Deep learning on whole-slide cytology for early lesion detection
    • Attention-based multiple-instance learning over whole-slide images with patient-aware splits and evaluation.
    • Multimodal RGB–hyperspectral fusion with uncertainty estimation for oral lesion classification.
    • Developed in close collaboration with pathologists to align model outputs with clinical reporting.
  • Capstone — Telemedicine Platform Jan 2025 — May 2025
    Neurology hospital management system
    • Architected a telemedicine web platform on Apache and PHP with role-based clinical workflows.
    • Integrated an AI-assisted image analysis module to support remote neurological diagnostics.
  • B.Tech Thesis — KGEC 2023 — 2024
    Pancreatic cancer prediction from imaging & biomarkers
    • Developed convolutional neural networks to automate pancreatic tumour detection from CT scans.
    • Implemented machine learning classifiers on clinical urinary biomarkers, surpassing the previous state-of-the-art accuracy by 0.7%.
