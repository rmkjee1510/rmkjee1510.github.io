Medical Image Computing & Inverse Problems

Hello! I’m Rupam. I recently completed my M.Tech in Medical Imaging & Informatics at the School of Medical Science & Technology, Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur. My thesis, advised by Prof. Nirmalya Ghosh (Department of Electrical Engineering), develops physics-aware learning for modulo sinogram unwrapping and CT reconstruction — embedding the forward physics of computed tomography directly into the learning pipeline. Alongside this, I work with Prof. Subhamoy Mandal (SMST) and Dr. Shirin Dasgupta (Pathologist) on oral pathological image analysis for the early detection of oral potentially malignant disorders.
